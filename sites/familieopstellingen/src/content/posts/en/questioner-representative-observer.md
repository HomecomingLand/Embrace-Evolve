There are three positions in constellation work: questioner, representative and observer. Each position offers you a specific, unique experience and is valuable for your own process.

## As a questioner

As a questioner you gain clarity about the dynamics of the question you brought. You look at your own system from the outside and see movements you could not see from within.

## As a representative

As a representative you open up to your felt experience. Your own pieces can be felt, clarified and deepened through representing for someone else's question. Often what you represent touches precisely on something of your own.

## As an observer

As an observer you have the room to stay very close to yourself and to feel the dynamics that unfold from your own energy. Even without standing in the field, something moves.

## From every position

From every position, constellations can touch your feelings deeply and give clear insights into your own themes, dynamics and questions. Even if you do not bring a question, or your question is not worked with, taking part in a family constellation can be a deeply healing experience.
