During family constellations we work from subtle energies and deep layers of your subconscious. This deepens and widens your awareness in the here and now.

## Beforehand

You are invited, without obligation, to connect beforehand with what is alive in you: your question, your theme, what you would like to see change. You do not need to write anything down or prepare. It is enough to pause and sit with it a few times in the days before the constellation.

You do not have to bring a question. Even if you have none, or your question is not worked with that day, taking part in a family constellation can be a deeply healing experience.

## How the day unfolds

We start with a welcome, so everyone can arrive. During the day itself we see which constellations need to come forward. Groups vary between 12 and 20 participants and we set up roughly a handful of questions.

## Why we set up "blind"

Constellations are set up "blind": representatives are invited into the field without knowing who or what they represent. This switches off the possibility of projection and creates room for representatives to stay close to what they feel.

No participant hears the question or its details without the questioner's consent. What you bring stays yours.

## Carrying the group together

A constellation day is a dynamic group process in which we depend on each other's presence. We therefore ask you not to cancel at short notice. Should you be unable to come after all, please let us know as soon as possible so we can give your place to someone else.
